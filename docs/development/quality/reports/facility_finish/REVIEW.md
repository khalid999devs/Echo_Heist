Khalid, 2026-10-22. User-directed follow-up to the [closer-camera revision](../close_camera/REVIEW.md), baseline main `9b308e4`. Source remains Python/pygame-ce; cinematic backgrounds are rendered illustrations. P01/P02 stay complete, P03 stays partial and P04-P08 remain open.

## What changed

The display now fills the actual window/desktop aspect ratio. A variable logical canvas preserves a centred 1280×720 interface safe area; backgrounds cover the display and gameplay gains the extra view area. Controls and characters retain their proportions. The shared pointer pipeline maps OS window coordinates to this canvas, then into the interface safe area once. Back, pause, settings, sliders and map use the same rectangles for drawing and hit testing. Resizing a paused mission preserves its fingerprint. Briefing labels/cards now have explicit vertical separation.

The same two decks, five zones and 14 rooms retain both branch alternatives, cross-link, stairs and return paths. The 96px tiles, larger distance-driven operative cycles and tight camera remain. Room equipment now differs by purpose: centrifuges, spectroscopy, fume cupboards, cold storage, vacuum assemblies, robotics, coolant and control consoles. The default one-seat console has two monitors and one keyboard/mouse. Appearance and condition are validated optional map metadata, separate from physical collision flags.

The takeover aftermath is selective: intact entry/checkpoint areas, abandoned analysis stations, cracked consoles, unfinished benches, displaced chairs, sample carts, loose papers, faint spills/scuffs and dusty service equipment. Some plants are healthy and others wilted. Six perimeter utility runs connect equipment visually. Decorative clutter does not invent a movement or sound obstacle. Fixtures are graded once and cached; active indicators are absent on broken/abandoned consoles. Soft sheared silhouette shadows preserve base width instead of rotating a complete table shadow diagonally.

Five hiding markers have authored recesses and open maintenance-cabinet artwork. Cover cabinets remain real movement/vision/sound obstacles. Reachability, nearby opaque cover and patrol separation are checked. Enter-hide/concealment, breaking glass, working doors and canister interactions remain P05 as explicitly agreed; walking into this artwork never grants or fakes invisibility.

Audio includes four more rhythmic, context-specific CC0 music tracks, retained result transmission, low ventilation, quieter filtered walking, distinctly louder sprinting, stair/core/recharge feedback and gentle menu/adjustment sounds. All 29 assets decode. Walk playback gain is 0.09 and sprint 0.42; simulation hearing remains one versus three tiles. A regression verifies the same distant walk is unheard while a sprint creates heard evidence and the Sentinel investigates on its next observation. Playback cannot alert guards or change Simulation. Foreign tactical cues are heard only when permitted by the operative's filtered observation. Pause, mute, separate volumes and scene exit govern ambience/siren lifecycle.

## Design references and provenance

The variable viewport follows pygame-ce's [display sizing API](https://pyga.me/docs/ref/display.html); full-screen dimensions come from the actual desktop. The equipment/cover arrangement adapts the level designer's [cover, sightline and route discussion](https://www.danielemascagna.com/level-design-of-die-young-prologue/) and Klei's [readability/iteration postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/classic-postmortem-klei-entertainment-s-i-mark-of-the-ninja-i-). These are design applications to this map, not a guarantee of difficulty or playtest consensus.

Packaged credits, original notices, hashes and per-file sources accompany the game. Exact image generation [prompts](PROMPTS.md) and [selected PNG hashes](verification/ASSETS.json) preserve the new art's provenance.

No scoring change or new phase completion is claimed. Companion/Workshop/Guide designs navigate correctly; missing autonomous controllers, NEXUS, policy persistence, final scoring and P05 interactions remain explicit future consumers.
