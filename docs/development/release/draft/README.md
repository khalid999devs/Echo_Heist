## Proposed itch.io page copy

**ECHO HEIST: The Core Rebellion**

Steal a dangerous experimental Core from Nexus Research and escape the lockdown. Choose the Security stairway or the Research route. Break sight at a corner, use a decoy, and pick your escape through Entry or Cargo.

Play as the Human operative or configure Echo and watch the autonomous Companion face the same defenders. Tune navigation, guard reaction, emergency response and risk in the Workshop. Compare scores and inspect actual decisions in the mission report.

**Desktop game.** Planned free downloads for Windows x64 and macOS Apple Silicon. Include the verified platform ZIPs, [controls](PLAYER_GUIDE.md), [credits](CREDITS.md) and a gameplay clip when the release gates pass.
