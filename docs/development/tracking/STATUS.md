The facility has two exit airlocks and six authored defenders, including two at the Core and one covering Cargo. After 0.5 seconds of sustained confirmed sight, pursuing guards can close the gap at 108% of the sprint speed. Break sight around furniture or use an unwitnessed locker or desk. Adjacent guards can complete a grab until reach is broken or a tool stops them. Both Human exits were verified on seed 1042. With memory off, Rush extracts on seed 44 and is caught on seed 18. The controlled comparison has 95 captures and one timeout across 96 runs. These scenarios and the stock deployable runs start at different positions, patrol timings and inventories.

The scoring weights are unchanged. Pace and Speedrunner thresholds await human playtest times. Suhita's earlier physical Windows QA includes partial gameplay and frozen smoke. It predates the new map/rules. The GitHub repository remains private. Free Windows x64 and Apple Silicon downloads on itch.io require the platform, licensing and publication gates in the release plan.
