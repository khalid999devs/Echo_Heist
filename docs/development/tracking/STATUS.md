The facility has two exit airlocks and six authored defenders: two Sentinels, two Trackers (Research and Vault) and two Interceptors (Core and Cargo), the second Interceptor being a user override of the blueprint's single one. Each Interceptor only holds gates in its own zone. A chasing guard moves at 125% of its walk; after 1 second of sustained confirmed sight it closes at 103% of the sprint speed, and it runs straight at a thief it can see across open floor. A guard that walks into an unseen body turns toward it. Break sight around furniture or use an unwitnessed locker or desk. Stunned or disabled guards no longer block movement. Adjacent guards can complete a grab until reach is broken or a tool stops them. In the presentation build v0.1.0a1, a gadget run on seed 1042 (two Echoes, EMP, both stuns, Cargo exit) wins with 76 points, rank A, hiding only while a guard is in view; the older quiet route is now caught by the Vault Tracker. With memory off, Rush extracts on seed 42 and is caught on seed 82. Over seeds 1 to 400 Rush wins twice and reaches the Core 75 times. The controlled comparison has 96 captures in 96 runs. A glitch probe over 540 Companion missions reports no stuck, jittering or looping actors. Version 0.1.0a2 retunes the Core room, described in [Core room balance](../planning/balance/CORE_ROOM.md): new Vault Tracker and Core Interceptor loops, Trackers that answer only nearby radio reports and walk at 2.0 tiles per second, normal-length lockdown searches and a more patient Companion. Over the same seeds the robots steal the Core 168 times instead of 84 and Rush wins on seeds 105, 164 and 301. The glitch probe flags 3 of 540 missions on the new routes. Version 0.1.0a3 gives the Companion a new planner and the game a light easing, described in [Companion planner](../planning/balance/COMPANION.md): guards keep their routes with a per-seed rhythm, the robot sees about one screen like a player, learns guard beats and plans each step with space-time A*. Over seeds 1 to 100 the robots steal the Core 132 times in 300 missions and win 30 times, against 3 wins in 1,200 before. `scripts/checks/run.py` passes with 373 tests. These scenarios and the stock deployable runs start at different positions, patrol timings and inventories.

The scoring weights are unchanged. Pace and Speedrunner thresholds await human playtest times. Suhita's earlier physical Windows QA includes partial gameplay and frozen smoke. It predates the new map/rules. The GitHub repository is public. [PR #13](https://github.com/khalid999devs/Echo_Heist/pull/13) adds the public README, the PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0 in `LICENSE.md` and the first GitHub release. Release 0.1.0a2 ships the Core room balance. Both are recorded in the [GitHub release record](../release/GITHUB_RELEASE.md). Free downloads on itch.io still need the remaining platform and publication gates in the release plan.
