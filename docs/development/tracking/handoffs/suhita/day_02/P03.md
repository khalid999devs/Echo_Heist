## What now works

- **Registry (SR3):** `level/security.py` validates every authored post/route/chokepoint and assigns roles: two Sentinels (`guard-service`, `guard-security`, BFS/FSM/fuzzy), one Tracker (`guard-research`, A*), and `guard-core` as a sensing-only **POST** until P04 adds its Interceptor minimax. `HeistSession` replaced the temporary nearest-Sentinel choice with this registry. `security_enabled=False` remains only a diagnostic fixture.
- **Tracker (SR2):** `ai/guards/tracker.py` identifies only by its own sight meter. It plans with `ai/navigation/astar.py` toward the visible tile, then to its own last-seen tile when contact breaks. There it searches up to four reachable tiles within two steps of the last sighting, ordered by the motion it actually observed, and returns once evidence expires (`memory_ticks`=150, about 5 s). A* uses a stair-portal heuristic that remains admissible and consistent across the two-deck atlas; stairs are taken through the existing Simulation INTERACT.
- **Radio provenance (SR1):** SIGHTING reports carry the reporter and original tick. The Tracker treats another guard's report as an INVESTIGATE lead (`evidence_kind=REPORT`); re-delivery cannot refresh its age or confirm identity. Every goal records `evidence_kind/id/tick`; Simulation rejects evidence absent from that guard's own Observation.
- **Cue expiry (SR1):** a Sentinel INVESTIGATE now expires from its cue's original tick instead of waiting forever at an unreachable origin. Heard cues still reveal only a tile.
- **Seen vs unseen hiding (SR1):** an intruder crouched inside an authored under-desk clearance is invisible to every guard. A controlled guard that saw the intruder on the previous tick receives a `HIDE_ENTRY` cue (and `HIDE_WITNESSED` event). Only that guard can reveal the actor, by inspecting from an adjacent tile within the cue's lifetime. An unseen entry stays hidden even when a guard patrols right beside the desk.
- **Capture fairness (SR1):** Sentinel/Tracker capture still requires CHASE plus current sight; a POST requires current sight; guards outside a registry keep P02 contact rules but can never grab a concealed intruder. Tools still resolve before capture.
- **Geometry revisions (SR3):** `Simulation.request_geometry_revision(map, reason)` validates a same-size, same-stairs map that still fits every actor, applies it at the next tick, bumps `Observation.geometry_revision` and emits `GEOMETRY_REVISED`. Guards replan on a new revision. No door state was added (P05).
- **Replay (SR3):** `core/simulation/replay.py` records a header (version, fingerprint schema 8, map/rules/sensor hashes, registry revision, seed, 30 Hz, mode) plus per-tick intents and event hashes, and verifies a re-run tick by tick and by final fingerprint.
