## Implemented behavior

**SR1 Companion** (`ai/companion/`, `ai/planning/mcts.py`, `ai/navigation/`)
- Mission FSM INFILTRATE → REACH_CORE → TAKE_CORE → EXTRACT (→ WIN / CAPTURED) through the same Simulation, actions, speeds, stairs, tools and stock as the Human. Knowledge is the public briefing (map, zones/chokepoints, cover, Core/Exit, guard roster with type and home sector) plus its own Observation. No hidden positions, recordings, Human paths or scripted QA fixtures.
- Utility overrides each tick: grabbed → melee/EMP/pull away per the Emergency block; adjacent watched guard → melee/EMP/escape. **Melee is planned only with the Companion's own observed `melee_charges > 0` and `melee_cooldown_remaining <= 1`** (commit-next-tick rule), plus per-target immunity.
- Strategic MCTS (64 iterations, depth 4, UCT, seeded): selection, one-action expansion, ADVANCE-biased playout, backpropagation of the normalized blueprint reward. Each iteration determinizes unseen guards from beliefs/briefed priors. Rollouts carry melee charges and recovery seconds (a strike spends one charge and starts the rules' recovery; every transition advances it by elapsed abstract time). The leaf estimate now charges every remaining sector the same exposure an actual crossing pays, so postponing a crossing is no longer free.
- Observed-risk A* (admissible, stair-aware) with nonnegative penalties for observed cones, visible bodies and jammed tiles.
- Policies: non-cautious policies (Balanced/Rush) sprint while infiltrating or carrying the Core and press on when spotted by a slower patrol; Cautious (Ghost) breaks line of sight. A non-grabbing guard that keeps the Companion in "escape" (e.g. the Interceptor holding a gate in lockdown), or a grab/escape alternation, marks that guard's tiles as jammed and forces a re-plan; ALTERNATE commits by excluding the abandoned route for 180 ticks.

**SR2 defenders** (`ai/guards/interceptor.py`, `ai/planning/minimax.py`, `ai/director/nexus.py`)
- Interceptor (Core guard identity, role `INTERCEPTOR`, label `ALPHA-BETA`): enumerates plausible escape routes from evidence (original tile/tick), searches depth-3 MIN post / MAX branch / MIN redeploy over its home gates with alpha-beta (identical value/post to plain minimax), then walks there with A*; holds during lockdown.
- NEXUS: evidence-limited view (guard-heard cues, guard-seen motion, radio reports, public phase/alarm, own robots). Prioritized tasks with caps and retained routine coverage; addressed `DISPATCH` reports keep the original evidence tick; one lockdown announcement.

**SR3 protocols/deployment** (`ai/director/protocols.py`, `infrastructure/persistence/protocols.py`, `autonomy/`)
- `NexusGateway` implements `AutonomyGateway`, advertising HUMAN and COMPANION with protocols. Human actions pass through unchanged; Companion mode ignores input.
- Schema-1 `nexus-memory.json` beside settings: missing → baseline, corrupt → quarantined, future schema → refused. Evidence recorded once per finished attempt; at most one protocol, announced before deployment and fixed for the attempt. Reset clears all inputs and reproduces the baseline fingerprint.
- Startup: `app` resolves the per-user data directory (also without `--data-dir`), and `StartupLoader` builds the gateway on the worker and injects `GameFlow(..., autonomy_gateway=gateway)` — Companion deployment works on first launch.

## Known limitations (recorded honestly)

- Policy outcomes differ substantially (see verification): Balanced is the strong default; Rush (shortest route, melee-first) and Ghost (cautious hiding) lose most seeds under the two-charge rule. Policy balancing is P06/P07 work.
- Guards can still body-block each other in a one-tile gate (pre-existing shared P03 steering). Rarely this leaves both guards and the Companion stalled (one Rush seed in the evidence runner); Ghost can also be slow enough to reach the runner's 9,000-tick cap. The game has no tick cap.
- Protocol memory has no settings toggle yet (P06).
