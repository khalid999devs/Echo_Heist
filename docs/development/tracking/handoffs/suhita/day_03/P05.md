## Implemented behavior

**SR2 environment** (`core/environment/`, `core/contracts/environment/model.py`, `level/environment.py`, map markers)
- Doors, breakable glass, loose canisters and lockers are authored map data with stable IDs and per-run state owned by Simulation. Three doors, two glass panes, three canisters and the five existing HideSpots (now lockers) are placed in the packaged facility.
- Door/glass changes update collision, sight and reach together through one geometry revision at the next tick; closed doors stay routable and open for anyone who pushes into them, so no route is ever permanently locked.
- Once-only sounds: one `GLASS` break per pane, one `METAL` impact per canister roll, a `DOOR` sound only for intruder-caused motion. Every cue carries its original tick and the real NoiseEvent ID; guards hear them through the existing acoustic rules (T03 with real glass).
- Lockers reuse the existing concealment/witness rules: unseen entry hides the intruder from sight and capture; a witnessed entry can be inspected.
- `PresentationSnapshot.environment` is filled from committed state, so Khalid's prop renderer, captions and spatial audio now receive real data.
