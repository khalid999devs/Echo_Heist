# Audio sources

All selected recordings are unmodified and offered by their authors under CC0 1.0. The full CC0 legal code is bundled as `CC0.txt`; Kenney's original notice is retained as `KENNEY_LICENSE.txt`. Exact filenames, source pages, download date, SHA256 and byte counts are in `manifest.json`.

- SRG774, [Dark Sci-Fi Audio Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/dark-sci-fi-audio-pack): title, sector ambience, pursuit pulse, urgent lockdown, transmission outcome music, hover and victory cues.
- rubberduck, [50 CC0 Sci-Fi SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/50-cc0-sci-fi-sfx): terminal confirmation, hologram deployment, electrical EMP and security alert tones.
- Kenney, [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), version 1.0: three concrete footstep variants and one metal impact for the emergency stun.

Sources and licenses checked/downloaded 2026-10-21. Credit is included voluntarily. These recordings are presentation assets: tactical sounds follow committed simulation events; playback does not create secret perception events. Music is deliberately quiet beneath footsteps and tool/security cues, with separate music/effect sliders.
