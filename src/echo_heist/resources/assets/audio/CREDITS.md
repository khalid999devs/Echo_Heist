# Audio sources

All selected recordings are offered by their authors under CC0 1.0. Original recordings remain unmodified except the explicitly documented low-pass walking variants; their sources and processing are recorded in the manifest. The full CC0 legal code is bundled as `CC0.txt`; Kenney's original notice is retained as `KENNEY_LICENSE.txt`. Exact filenames, source pages, download date, SHA256 and byte counts are in `manifest.json`.

- SRG774, [Dark Sci-Fi Audio Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/dark-sci-fi-audio-pack): transmission outcome music, ventilation bed, hover and victory cues.
- rubberduck, [50 CC0 Sci-Fi SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/50-cc0-sci-fi-sfx): terminal confirmation, hologram deployment, electrical EMP and security alert tones.
- Kenney, [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), version 1.0: three concrete footstep variants and one metal impact for the emergency stun.

Sources and licenses checked/downloaded 2026-10-21. Credit is included voluntarily. These recordings are presentation assets: tactical sounds follow committed simulation events; playback does not create secret perception events. Music is deliberately quiet beneath footsteps and tool/security cues, with separate music/effect sliders.

2026-10-22 scene/audio refinement: four original OGG tracks from Alexander Ehlers / tricksntraps, [T & T Free Cyberpunk Pack 2](https://opengameart.org/content/t-t-free-cyberpunk-pack-2), replace title/infiltration/pursuit/lockdown music. Exact filenames and hashes are in the manifest. Transmission retains SRG774; the former sector bed is reused as low-level ventilation. Kenney [Interface Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) adds quiet adjustment/back/toggle, stair start/arrival, core, recharge, unavailable-action and visible-suspicion cues; its original notice is in `notices/KENNEY_INTERFACE.txt`. Three walking WAVs derive from the existing Kenney footsteps with a 2400 Hz low-pass filter; sprint keeps the original recordings at a distinctly higher playback gain. Cosmetic ambience/UI playback never emits a simulation noise.
